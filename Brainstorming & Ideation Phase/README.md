# Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

## Project Title
PocketSmart AI: Your Smart Budget & Recommendation Assistant

## Idea
PocketSmart AI is a smart budget and recommendation assistant designed to help users plan their budget for different needs.

## Main Idea
The system provides simple budget planning and budget allocation recommendations for:
- Home Interior
- Party Planning
- Jewelry Planning

## Objective
To help users manage their budget easily and make suitable budget allocation decisions.
